# Pilot Prompts - Nano Banana 2

## Global art direction
- Use case: illustration-story
- Asset type: game card or enemy illustration
- Style/medium: premium digital painting, semi-realistic, dramatic, cinematic, fantasy TCG mood grounded in football
- Composition/framing: horizontal panoramic illustration for the upper box of a trading card or enemy portrait panel, one clear focal action, readable silhouette at small size
- Lighting/mood: stadium floodlights, dramatic contrast, atmospheric depth, premium painterly finish
- Constraints: no text, no logos, no frame, no watermark added manually, no cartoon, no anime, no chibi, no extra limbs
- Avoid: generic sports photo, modern poster design, oversaturated neon, cheesy lens flare, cluttered background, medieval fantasy with no football
